# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The **Oficina de Tecnologías de la Información (OTI)** of the
**Universidad Nacional de San Cristóbal de Huamanga (UNSCH)** is
committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, harassment-free
collaboration environment for everyone involved in the design,
development, operation, and study of the university's information
systems.

This Code of Conduct applies to all members of the UNSCH community who
participate in OTI-managed spaces: students, faculty, alumni,
administrative staff, contractors, interns, visitors, and external
collaborators.

We pledge to make participation in our projects and communities a
respectful and professional experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-
economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Empathy and respect** — Treating every person with courtesy and
  professional consideration.
- **Constructive feedback** — Receiving and giving technical review in
  a way that improves the work and the team.
- **Inclusive language** — Communicating clearly and respectfully in
  Spanish, Quechua, English, or any other language used in our
  spaces.
- **Collaboration** — Supporting the learning and growth of other
  community members.
- **Data stewardship** — Handling personal and institutional data
  with the highest level of care, in line with Peruvian Law N.° 29733.
- **Accountability** — Owning our mistakes, learning from them, and
  making amends where appropriate.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- **Harassment or discrimination** in any form — verbal, written, or
  visual — including sexist, racist, homophobic, transphobic,
  ableist, or otherwise discriminatory jokes or language.
- **Sexualised language or imagery** in repositories, comments,
  commit messages, Pull Requests, issues, or institutional
  communication channels.
- **Intimidation, stalking, or trolling**, including personal attacks
  or sustained disruption of discussions.
- **Public or private retaliation** against a person who reports a
  concern in good faith.
- **Disclosure of private information** ("doxing") of community
  members or of UNSCH stakeholders without their explicit consent.
- **Misuse of institutional resources**, including the use of OTI
  platforms for personal, political, or commercial purposes unrelated
  to the university's mission.
- **Any conduct that would be incompatible with the Statutes of
  UNSCH** or with the Code of Ethics of the Peruvian public service.

---

## Maintainers' Responsibilities

The maintainers of OTI repositories and the OTI leadership are
responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable
behaviour. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behaviour that they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and the responsibility to **remove, edit,
or reject** comments, commits, Pull Requests, issues, and other
contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and
will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by OTI — UNSCH,
including:

- Source-code repositories under the `OTI-UNSCH` organization on
  GitHub (or any other forge operated by OTI).
- Issue trackers, Pull Requests, project boards, and Discussions.
- Institutional communication channels (mailing lists, instant-
  messaging groups, video calls) used for OTI work.
- Workshops, training sessions, hackathons, and any in-person or
  virtual event organised by, or on behalf of, OTI.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour
may be reported to the OTI Conduct Team:

- **Email:** [oti@unsch.edu.pe](mailto:oti@unsch.edu.pe)
- **Subject line:** `[Code of Conduct] Incident report`
- **Encryption (optional):** PGP key available on request from the
  same address.

All reports will be:

- Handled **promptly** and **confidentially**.
- Investigated by a panel of at least two OTI staff members with no
  conflict of interest in the case.
- Documented internally, with outcomes recorded and (where required)
  escalated to the corresponding UNSCH authority (Rectorado, Tribunal
  Universitario, Defensoría Universitaria, or the competent external
  authority).

The privacy and safety of the reporter will be protected. Retaliation
against a reporter in good faith is itself a violation of this Code.

### What to include in a report

To help us act quickly, please include:

1. **Your contact information** (so we can follow up).
2. **Names of individuals involved**, if known.
3. **A description of the incident**, with date, time, and location
   (channel, repository, event).
4. **Any supporting evidence** (links, screenshots) — do not include
   anything you are not comfortable sharing.
5. **Whether you wish to remain anonymous** in our internal records.

---

## Conflict resolution

Not every disagreement is a Code of Conduct issue. For ordinary
technical disagreements, please use the normal review and discussion
channels first. The Code of Conduct exists to protect people, not to
silence disagreement.
